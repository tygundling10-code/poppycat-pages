How to buy

No cart, no checkout. Just say hello.

  1. Find your piece

    Browse the work and pick the original or print you'd like to take home.

  2. Send a message

    Email me or message me on Instagram with the name of the piece. I'll confirm it's available and set it aside for you.

  3. Pay by e-Transfer

    Send an Interac e-Transfer for the price of the piece. Once it arrives, it's officially yours.

  4. Pickup or shipping

    Pickup or shipping, whichever suits you. I'll confirm any shipping cost before you pay.
